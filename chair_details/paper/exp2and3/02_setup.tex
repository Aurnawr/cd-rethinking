\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:cdmech-setup}

\subsection{What the experiments do}
\label{subsec:cdmech-what}

Both are \emph{observational} instrumentations of a normal CD run: the decoding
algorithm is unaltered, we only record its internal state at every step.

\textbf{Exp.~2 (Intervention rate; H1).} At each step $t$ we record the token CD
emits, $y_t$, and the expert's own greedy choice, $\arg\max_w E_t(w)$, and
report how often they coincide --- over all steps, and restricted to steps
emitting a \textbf{real} or \textbf{hallucinated} object. Agreement means CD
changed nothing.

\emph{Scope.} Since this instruments a real CD decode, $E_t$ is teacher-forced
on \emph{CD's own prefix} $y_{<t}$. The question is: \emph{holding history fixed
at what CD actually generated, would plain greedy differ at this step?} It is
not a sequence-level claim about matching an independent greedy rollout. The
step-level framing isolates the contrastive step's contribution at the moment it
acts; a sequence-level comparison would conflate it with compounding
autoregressive divergence. It is also conservative: any argmax difference counts
as an intervention, however inconsequential.

\textbf{Exp.~3 (Candidate overlap; H2).} At each step we record the top-$10$
under three rankings --- expert $E_t$, amateur $A_t$, contrastive
$C_t=(1{+}\alpha)E_t-\alpha A_t$ (pre-APC) --- and measure (i) \textbf{set
overlap} (same words, order-insensitive) and (ii) \textbf{exact-position match}.
The pair separates \emph{``do the branches consider the same words?''} from
\emph{``do they order them the same way?''}

\subsection{Models, data, decoding}
\label{subsec:cdmech-models}

\textbf{Models} (both \texttt{float16}, \texttt{sdpa}): \textbf{LLaVA-1.5-7B}
(CLIP ViT-L/14-336px $\to$ linear projector $\to$ Vicuna-7B-v1.5; fixed $576$
image tokens; \texttt{vicuna\_v1} template; LLaMA/SentencePiece tokenizer, vocab
$32$k, word-initial marker \texttt{\_}) and \textbf{Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct}
(dynamic-resolution ViT, $2\times2$ merging, 3D mRoPE; visual-token count is
image-dependent, $\approx266$ at \texttt{min\_pixels}$=256\cdot28\cdot28$ /
\texttt{max\_pixels}$=1280\cdot28\cdot28$; \texttt{IMAGE\_TOKEN\_ID}$=151655$;
chat template; byte-level BPE, vocab $151{,}643$, word-initial sub-tokens carry
a leading space).

\textbf{Data.} $500$ MSCOCO \texttt{val2017} images --- the \emph{same ids, same
order}, for every method and both models (verified), so all comparisons are
paired. $500$ is the conventional CHAIR sample size~\citep{huang2024opera}. We
deviate from the prevailing \texttt{val2014} convention; this shifts absolute
CHAIR values but no within-study comparison, since every method sees identical
images. Ground truth is standard CHAIR~\citep{rohrbach2018chair}: COCO
instance-segmentation categories $\cup$ objects named in the five reference
captions, mapped to MSCOCO-80 via the CHAIR synonym dictionary. Prompt:
\emph{``Describe this image in detail.''}

\textbf{Decoding.} Greedy (\texttt{do\_sample=False}), \texttt{max\_new\_tokens}
$=256$; determinism isolates the contrastive mechanism from sampling noise.
Following the reference implementation,
\begin{equation}
  C_t(w) = (1+\alpha)E_t(w) - \alpha A_t(w),
  \qquad
  \mathcal{V}_{\text{head}} = \big\{ w : E_t(w) \ge \log\beta + \max_{w'} E_t(w') \big\},
\end{equation}
with $\alpha=1.0$, $\beta=0.2$; tokens outside $\mathcal{V}_{\text{head}}$ are
set to $-\infty$ before the $\arg\max$. \textbf{The APC cutoff depends on the
expert logits only.} The greedy baseline is plain $\arg\max_w E_t(w)$.

\textbf{VCD.} Amateur $=$ diffusion-noised image: $T=1000$,
$\beta_{1:T}=\sigma(\mathrm{linspace}(-6,6,1000))\cdot(0.5\!\times\!10^{-2}-10^{-5})+10^{-5}$,
$\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{s\le t}(1-\beta_s)$,
$v'=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_T}v+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_T}\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$,
at \texttt{noise\_step}$=500$; drawn once per image, fixed across that caption's
steps. All \S\ref{sec:cdmech-results} numbers use $500$; analyses elsewhere in this
paper at other noise levels are separate experiments, labelled at point of use.

\textbf{SID.} Reference recipe: \texttt{AGG\_LAYER}$=2$,
\texttt{ATTENTION\_RANK}$=72$ of $576$ (a \emph{random} $12.5\%$ subset of image
tokens is \emph{kept}, drawn once per image); layers $0$--$1$ see the full
image, layer $\ge2$ masks all non-kept image tokens. On LLaVA this is the
vendored \texttt{use\_sid} path. On Qwen2.5-VL we reproduce it by per-layer
mask injection under \texttt{sdpa}, because \texttt{eager} attention is
numerically broken for Qwen2.5-VL in \texttt{transformers}~5.x (it emits
degenerate text), making the original eager-mask hook unusable: at layers
$\ge$\texttt{AGG\_LAYER} we inject a causal 4D additive mask setting non-kept
image key-columns to $-\infty$. Verified to change amateur logits vs.\ no
masking ($\overline{|\Delta|}=0.77$ over the expert's top-$30$) \emph{and} to
differ from all-layer masking ($\overline{|\Delta|}=2.01$), confirming layers
$0$--$1$ stay unmasked. Qwen applies the same $72/576$ \emph{fraction} of that
image's (dynamic) visual tokens.

\subsection{Instrumentation and labelling}
\label{subsec:cdmech-capture}

At every step we log the top-$30$ ids and logits for expert, amateur,
contrastive (pre-APC) and post-APC scores, plus the APC cutoff, emitted token id
and per-candidate APC-survival flag: $60{,}094$/$59{,}271$ steps for LLaVA
(VCD/SID), $93{,}652$/$94{,}081$ for Qwen. We store \textbf{ids and logits
only} --- no token-level semantic labels --- so object attribution happens
downstream at the caption level, which structurally prevents sub-word
mislabelling.

\textbf{Object attribution.} Labels come from standard CHAIR applied to the
\emph{generated caption text}, never to individual sub-word tokens: words are
singularized, multi-word categories collapsed (\emph{``teddy bear''},
\emph{``dining table''}), and matched to MSCOCO-80 through the synonym
dictionary (\emph{man/woman/guy} $\to$ \texttt{person}). A mention is
\textbf{real} iff its category is in the image's ground truth, else
\textbf{hallucinated}. Each mention maps back to the step producing its first
token via incremental detokenization.

This discipline is not cosmetic, and we flag it because it changed our own
conclusions. Our first pass labelled each decoding step by string-matching the
token emitted at it. That matcher scores the \emph{``ship''} inside
\emph{``companionship''} as a boat, splits \emph{``bear''} from \emph{``teddy
bear''} and counts it as the animal, and silently misses any object whose name
the tokenizer splits. Under those labels, LLaVA's CD-vs-greedy agreement at
hallucinated-object steps reads $87.0\%$ (VCD) and $81.3\%$ (SID); under
caption-level CHAIR it is $75.1\%$ and $73.1\%$. The two are not the same
quantity measured twice: the per-token pass ranges over $384$/$364$ individual
\emph{tokens} tagged by string match, the corrected pass over $595$/$577$
caption-level object \emph{mentions}. The earlier figures were computed on an
incoherent unit and we report them only to document the correction; every number
in this paper uses the caption-level procedure, for both models. Qwen2.5-VL was
labelled at the caption level from the outset and is unaffected.

\subsection{Uncertainty}
\label{subsec:cdmech-uncertainty}

All intervals are \textbf{image-level (cluster) bootstrap} $95\%$ CIs,
$B=10{,}000$. Steps are not i.i.d.: they nest within captions within images, and
images differ systematically (a cluttered image yields both more hallucinations
and different agreement behaviour). A per-step Wilson/$z$ interval ignores this
clustering and can understate uncertainty. We therefore resample the $500$
\textbf{images} with replacement --- the item-level unit used elsewhere in this
paper --- and recompute each statistic from resampled totals; since every
quantity is a ratio of sums ($\sum_i \text{hall\_eq}_i / \sum_i
\text{hall\_n}_i$), this correctly weights images by their number of relevant
steps. The real-vs-hallucinated gap is tested by the same bootstrap (reported
$p$ is the two-sided proportion of resamples where the gap changes sign).
Clustering proves to matter little --- LLaVA-VCD's hallucinated-step interval is
$[71.4, 78.8]$ against a naive Wilson $[71.5, 78.4]$ --- but we report the
bootstrap regardless, since its validity does not depend on the effect being
large enough to survive the wrong analysis.

\subsection{Environment and limitations}
\label{subsec:cdmech-limits}

\textbf{Environment.} \texttt{PyTorch}~2.8.0+cu128,
\texttt{transformers}~5.12.1, \texttt{qwen-vl-utils}; one NVIDIA L4 (24\,GB).
LLaVA runs both branches in a manual KV-cached loop; Qwen caches the expert via
\texttt{generate()} but recomputes the amateur \emph{cache-less} each step,
because a hand-rolled mRoPE second-branch cache diverged from the exact
computation by up to $5.6$ logits (mean $2.8$) --- far beyond \texttt{fp16}
noise --- so we kept the correct-by-construction path. Caching is a speed
optimization only and does not change computed logits. Checks confirm the
amateur is bit-exactly deterministic under a fixed noise draw
($\max|\Delta|=0$) and that interleaving it does not corrupt the expert (an
independent teacher-forced pass reproduces captured expert logits to
$\overline{|\Delta|}\approx0.5$ on logits of magnitude $15$--$25$, the known
\texttt{fp16} cached-vs-uncached tolerance; cached values are the ecologically
valid ones, since deployed CD runs cached). \textbf{No global seed was fixed}:
VCD's noise draw and SID's kept subset are stochastic by construction, as in the
reference implementations, so per-image realizations differ across runs. All
quantities aggregate over $\ge59{,}000$ steps on the same fixed $500$ images, a
regime where this variance is negligible against the reported effects.

\textbf{Limitations.} (1) Logit statistics use the top-$30$, not the full
vocabulary --- exact for APC-survivor counts (APC retains $1$--$2$ tokens,
always within the top-$30$) but candidate-level statistics ignore the tail.
(2) CHAIR's ground truth is incomplete: an object present but neither segmented
nor mentioned in any reference caption scores as a hallucination. (3) Only
MSCOCO-80 categories are scored, since ground truth exists only for them. All
three apply \emph{identically} to every method and both models, so they cannot
bias the paired comparisons carrying our claims.
